Community Support

Get Support at Kandelia

We partner with newly arrived immigrant and refugee youth, adult learners, and families across King County with free, culturally affirming educational, digital, and emergency navigation programs.

Our Four Pillars

Comprehensive Support Programs

Every program is designed around active listening, language access, and whole-family empowerment.

Ages 14–21

High School Pathways & Youth Leadership

Dedicated after-school tutoring, ELL credit recovery, bilingual homework clubs, and post-secondary planning inside King County public high schools.

  • 1-on-1 academic mentoring & English conversation practice
  • Graduation audit & college / vocational application support
  • Paid summer youth leadership stipends and community projects
Enroll Student → Free for public school students
Parents & Adults

Family Digital Literacy & Tech Navigation

Hands-on computer workshops designed for adult caregivers to navigate school district portals (The Source), email, digital banking, and job applications.

  • Free Wi-Fi hotspots and subsidized home computer setups
  • Multilingual adult ESL cohorts in evening and Saturday sessions
  • Internet safety, parent advocacy, and healthcare navigation
Join Family Workshop → Bilingual instructors
Direct Aid

Emergency Relief & Housing Stabilization

Rapid, barrier-free direct cash assistance, emergency rental relief, and grocery support helping families maintain stability during unexpected hardship.

  • Over $240,000 disbursed directly to local newcomer households
  • Eviction prevention, utility assistance, and winter clothing aid
  • Confidential referral coordination with no documentation barriers
Request Relief Aid → Confidential intake
Wellness & Voice

Healing Circles & Cultural Affirmation

Safe, supportive community circles where youth and caregivers explore identity, navigate resettlement stress, and celebrate cultural traditions together.

  • Culturally responsive peer mentoring and storytelling spaces
  • Creative arts, cultural celebrations, and civic advocacy
  • Connection to licensed trauma-informed multilingual therapists
Join a Healing Circle → Warm, welcoming spaces
Simple & Barrier-Free

Program Eligibility Guidelines

All Kandelia programs are 100% free of charge. We warmly welcome immigrants, refugees, asylees, and first-generation families.

You are eligible for our programs if:

  • You or a family member are an immigrant, refugee, asylee, or humanitarian parolee residing in King County.
  • For youth programs: You are between the ages of 14 and 21 and enrolled in a King County middle or high school.
  • For adult workshops: You are a parent, caregiver, or adult seeking English language or digital literacy skills.
  • All languages, countries of origin, and immigration documentation statuses are embraced without barrier or disclosure requirements.
Need assistance or have questions? Call our intake line at 206-760-1573 or email help@kandelia.org.
