How Your Gift Directly Transforms Lives

Choose a giving level that resonates with your heart. Derived directly from our community pledge guidelines, every dollar creates immediate, tangible opportunities for immigrant and refugee youth and families in Seattle.

Diverse Giving Options

More Meaningful Ways to Support Kandelia

From workplace matching gifts to donor-advised funds, there are many flexible pathways to advance refugee and immigrant opportunities in our region.

Monthly Sustaining Giving

Joining our monthly Flourishing Circle creates reliable, predictable funding that keeps classrooms staffed, tutoring active, and emergency funds open 365 days a year.

Employer Matching Gifts

Double or triple your impact! Major employers (Microsoft, Boeing, Starbucks, Google, Gates Foundation) match charitable gifts 1:1. Request a match through your company portal.

Donor-Advised Funds (DAF) & Wire

Recommend a grant from your DAF (Seattle Foundation, Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard) or make direct institutional wire transfers to receive optimal tax benefits while uplifting our communities.

Appreciated Stock & Endowments

Donate appreciated securities or sponsor our landmark 50th Anniversary Celebration in May 2027. Elevate long-term equity, leadership pathways, and organizational stability.
