Executive Summary

Over the past year, Kandelia deepened its commitment to holistic, culturally responsive support for newly arrived immigrant and refugee families across King County. Working hand-in-hand with educators at Seattle World School, multilingual family advocates, and community partners, we removed systemic barriers so students and parents could thrive together.

Through after-school tutoring, Running Start college readiness coaching, paid youth internships, and direct emergency relief, 95% of participating seniors graduated on time—many becoming the first in their families to enter university.
